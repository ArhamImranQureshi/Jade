Skin Health Journal / Skin Tips

Skin tips that actually matter

Less noise, more consistency: six principles we come back to with almost every client.

Skin Tips · 3 min read

1. Get your skin explained

Many people own a drawer of products they hardly use. A skin analysis and consultation shows what your skin actually needs, so you can stop guessing.

2. Think in courses, not moments

Lasting skin confidence is rarely created in a single appointment. It develops through knowledge, consistency and clinical guidance.

3. Look at the inside too

Skin health is influenced by nutrition, gut health and the skin microbiome. Where appropriate, targeted nutritional support can work alongside your skincare.

4. Protect your barrier and keep it hydrated

A healthy barrier and good hydration underpin almost every skin goal, from texture to tone.

5. Keep a record

Your Skin Passport records treatments, skincare recommendations and progress, so your plan can be refined as your skin improves.

6. Be wary of trends

We choose treatments with purpose, not because they are fashionable. If something is right for your skin, we will tell you; if it is not, we will say that too.

This article is general information and is not a substitute for a consultation. Treatments are recommended individually after an assessment.
